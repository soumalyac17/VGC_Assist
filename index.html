META-INDEPENDENT TEAM BUILDING

Build with a reason.

VGC-Assist explores explainable, role-based reasoning for competitive Pokémon team construction. Rather than relying primarily on usage statistics, it aims to explain how each Pokémon contributes to a team's structure.

Explore the project ↓

THE IDEA

Three questions. One team.

01 / BUILD

Find the missing pieces

Explore candidates that may fill structural gaps in a partially completed team.

02 / SCORE

Evaluate the structure

Assess roles, coverage, speed, strategy, win conditions, synergy, and redundancy.

03 / EXPLAIN

Understand the reasoning

Show why a recommendation helps, what it costs, and what gaps remain.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Complex reasoning.
Simple presentation.

  • Explainable by designRecommendations connect to readable reasons.
  • Meta-independentFocus on team structure, not popularity rankings.
  • Role-basedEvaluate what a Pokémon contributes to this team.
  • Built to evolveSeparate objective Pokémon facts from strategic interpretation.
